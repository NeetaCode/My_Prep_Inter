AI SOFTWARE ENGINEER INTERVIEW QUESTIONS
1. Explain your thesis project and the biggest technical challenge you solved
My thesis focuses on Adaptive Retrieval Augmented Prompting (ARAP), a method designed to improve mathematical reasoning in large language models. I built a dual retrieval system that combines Example RAG, Formula RAG, and Chain of Thought prompting.
The main challenge was achieving consistent numerical accuracy from open source LLMs, which often produce unstable answers.
To solve this, I designed structured prompts with explicit variable mapping, built a MiniMathKB for formula retrieval, and improved search quality by combining BM25 with MiniLM embeddings. I also used repeat decoding and consistency checking to reduce hallucinations and stabilize final answers.

2. You improved LLM accuracy by up to 35 percent. What contributed the most?
Several techniques had a significant impact:
•	Better retrieval quality using BM25 combined with MiniLM embeddings
•	Structured prompts that clearly separated knowns, unknowns, and goals
•	Injecting mathematical formulas from MiniMathKB to anchor reasoning
•	Explicit final answer tagging to reduce hallucination
•	Decomposition prompts that guided the model through multi step problems
These changes strengthened reasoning reliability and reduced typical LLM errors.
